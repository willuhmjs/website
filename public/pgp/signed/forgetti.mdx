---
title: "The Forgetti Project"
description: "AI-driven failure detection for 3D printers."
date: "May 1 2024"
---

Forgetti is an AI-driven software project that detects print failures on 3D printers in real time and responds to them, so a failed print doesn't waste hours of material and machine time.

## What it does

Forgetti watches a print as it happens, spots a failure, and addresses it instead of letting the printer keep going.

## How it was built

I trained the detection models using high-performance computing resources at Old Dominion University, including its GPU clusters. The project received a $6,000 research grant, which funded the work from January 2024 through May 2025.
